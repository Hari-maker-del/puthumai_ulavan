# Puthumai Ulavan — Brand Guide

## Brand foundation

**Brand name:** Puthumai Ulavan (புதுமை உழவன்)  
**Category:** Farmer-first digital farming companion  
**Primary audience:** Indian farmers, starting with Tamil-speaking farmers and expanding carefully to other languages and regions.  
**Brand promise:** Practical farm information and digital tools that help farmers consider their next decision with more clarity.  
**Tagline (Tamil):** உழவரின் அறிவுக்கு, தொழில்நுட்பத்தின் துணை.  
**Tagline (English):** Technology that supports farmers.

## Positioning

Puthumai Ulavan helps farmers bring farm details, crop planning, weather information, expense records and available farming guidance into one place. It supports the farmer's judgement; it does not replace field experience, agricultural officers or qualified experts.

### What makes the brand different

- **Farmer-first:** Start with the farmer's problem, not the technology.
- **Practical:** Explain what a tool helps the farmer do in plain language.
- **Respectful:** Treat farmers as knowledgeable decision-makers, never as people who need to be talked down to.
- **Trustworthy:** Be clear about uncertainty, data use, pricing and what is actually available.
- **Locally aware:** Prioritize Tamil language and familiar agricultural context, while validating translations and recommendations with knowledgeable speakers and experts.

## Brand voice

Use warm, direct, plain language. Prefer a question a farmer might ask, followed by a clear explanation.

**Say:** “Track seed, fertilizer and labour expenses in one place.”  
**Avoid:** “Revolutionary AI-driven farm finance optimization.”

**Say:** “Explore crop suggestions based on the details you provide.”  
**Avoid:** “Guaranteed higher yield with AI.”

**Say:** “Use this insight alongside what you observe in the field.”  
**Avoid:** “The AI always knows the right answer.”

## Visual identity

- **Primary deep green:** #28633B — trust, growth, agriculture.
- **Deep forest:** #163B28 — headings and grounded brand moments.
- **Fresh green:** #3C874B — key emphasis, not every element.
- **Warm field neutral:** #F5F7F2 — calm background.
- **Earth accent:** #806A35 — restrained highlights.
- **White:** #FFFFFF — clear content surfaces.
- **Body text:** #56665A — comfortable reading.

Use generous whitespace, readable type, consistent corner radii and real agricultural imagery. Avoid neon green, excessive gradients, generic AI-brain visuals, fake dashboards, stock-photo overload and clutter.

## Logo and naming rules

- Use the supplied logo asset consistently where it remains legible.
- Keep the Tamil name **புதுமை உழவன்** and English name **Puthumai Ulavan** consistent across the site, app, social profiles and pitch materials.
- Do not stretch, recolor or add effects to the logo without an approved variant.
- Use the same name and tagline in the website header, footer, product sign-in screens and launch materials.

## Homepage messaging order

1. State the farmer value proposition and show a clear **Create your farm profile** action.
2. Present real farmer problems: crop planning, weather, crop health, expenses and harvest planning.
3. Explain how the platform works in three understandable steps.
4. Build trust with transparent limitations, privacy, support and FAQs.
5. Explain pricing only when the actual features, limits and payment behavior are confirmed.
6. Provide clear Sign in and Sign up navigation throughout.

## Trust rules for a startup

- Never invent farmer counts, testimonials, ratings, yield gains, savings, partnerships or certifications.
- Never imply that AI can guarantee crop yield, profit, weather or disease diagnosis.
- Do not call weather information “live” unless a real weather data source is connected and freshness is verified.
- Do not advertise a language, offline mode, feature or service until it works end-to-end.
- Publish a clear privacy notice before collecting farm location, contact details or other personal data.
- Test Tamil content with Tamil-speaking farmers; machine-translated text alone is not enough for important agricultural advice.
- Make pricing, free-tier limits, cancellation and data handling understandable before asking for payment.

## Suggested launch message

**Puthumai Ulavan — Technology that supports farmers.**  
Your crops, farm records and practical tools in one place. Start with your farm details, explore the information available to you, and make decisions with your own experience at the centre.

## Brand success measures

Measure meaningful outcomes rather than vanity metrics:
- Visitors who understand the product without assistance.
- Sign-up completion and successful farm-profile setup.
- Farmers who return to use a relevant tool.
- Help requests and where users become confused.
- Farmer feedback on Tamil clarity, usefulness and trust.
- Accuracy, freshness and reliability of the data behind each feature.
