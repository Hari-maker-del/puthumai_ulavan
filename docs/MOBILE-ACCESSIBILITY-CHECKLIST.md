# Mobile & Accessibility Checklist

Test 320px, 375px, 768px, 1024px and desktop.

- Every input has a visible or programmatic label.
- Icon-only controls have `aria-label`.
- Focus indicators remain visible.
- Touch targets are comfortable.
- Dialogs restore focus.
- Important errors use `role=alert`.
- Images have meaningful alt text or are explicitly decorative.
- Essential chart information has a textual alternative.
- Camera/microphone denial has a graceful fallback.
- Tamil and English text does not clip or overflow.
