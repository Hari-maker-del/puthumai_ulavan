# 5-Minute Puthumai Uzhavan Demo

1. Register/login as a farmer.
2. Open the farm profile and show the Farm Digital Twin context.
3. Show crop stage and today's weather.
4. Ask the AI Farm Brain a crop-management question.
5. Show the recommendation's reasons, evidence, confidence, and provenance.
6. Record an expense.
7. Show market/profit context.
8. Trigger or view a smart alert.
9. Record whether the recommendation was followed.
10. Open outcomes/history and show the decision → action → outcome loop.
11. Briefly demonstrate offline behavior.
12. Finish with the farm report.

The demo should use realistic data and visibly distinguish demo/estimated values from live data.
